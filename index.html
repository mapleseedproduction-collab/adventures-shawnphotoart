Great Divide Mountain Bike Ride (GDMBR)

The Great Divide Mountain Bike Ride follows my attempts to bikepack the Continental Divide from Banff, Alberta, to the U.S.–Mexico border. Through photographs and daily stories, these pages document the challenges, friendships, setbacks, and unforgettable experiences I encountered along the way.
