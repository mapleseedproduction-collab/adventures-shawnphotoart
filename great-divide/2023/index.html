Great Divide Mountain Bike Route

2023 Attempt

My first attempt at the Great Divide was in 2023. The ride ended after a crash south of Helena, Montana, on Day 12. I came back and finished the route in 2025.

Day-by-day journal from the 2023 attempt, drawn from the ride posts as written on the trail.

  1. Day 1
    Day 1: the first official day of my trail Tour.. cramps, Rain, and bears oh my

    11 June 2023

  2. Day 2
    Day 2: Wet morning Coffee, big climb, more bears, horses, trail Angels. This trail is full of surprises!!

    12 June 2023

  3. Day 3
    Day 3: Nice road ride, Hurt bear, bike repairs, hot tub, steak and beer

    12 June 2023

  4. Day 4
    Day 4: dear, Best burgers in British Columbia, Borders

    13 June 2023

  5. Day 5
    Day 5: salmon Florentine, little guardians, Big climbs, lake views

    14 June 2023

  6. Day 6
    Day 6: rain on a big downhill, Quick town stop, Montana mountain country roads, trail Angels

    15 June 2023

  7. Day 7
    Day 7: early start to coffee, road mistake, bikeing level up, hot chow

    17 June 2023

  8. Day 8
    Day 8: sleeping in, short day to no hotel, quick post

    18 June 2023

  9. Day 9
    Day 9: rain, small Town museum, Mountain bluebirds, and more big hills

    18 June 2023

  10. Day 10
    Day 10: later start, The llama ranch, Green meadows, Big city?!, One more section knocked out

    19 June 2023

  11. Day 11
    Day 11: Holliday, Zero day in Helena

    20 June 2023

  12. Day 12
    Day 12: Rockstarr BBQ, New gears, hospital trip.. sadly my ride is over, for now
